🎙️ **DrovaVoice – voice acting for Drova** 🎙️

⚠️ **All voices are AI-generated** (made with Fish Audio). No human recorded these lines.

I built a mod that gives Drova spoken dialogue. Characters speak their lines while the subtitles run as usual.

**Ready to play: German**
• Over 15,000 voiced lines in German
• Every named character has their own fixed voice
• Remarks in the world get quieter the further away you are
• Unnamed characters (guards, workers …) get a fitting voice from a small pool
• Volume and distance adjustable in the options menu

📦 German download (about 550 MB): https://github.com/janphi001/DrovaTTAI/releases/latest

**Want another language?**
The game has text in English, Spanish, French, Polish, Korean and Chinese as well. The generator on GitHub lets you create the voices for any of these yourself. You need your own Fish Audio account and a bit of time. The English guide is here: https://github.com/janphi001/DrovaTTAI/blob/main/README.en.md

**Not voiced**
• Your own answer options stay silent, like in the original
• Letters

**Installation**
You need MelonLoader 0.7.3 and the Drova Modding API 0.5.4. Then copy the `Mods` and `UserLibs` folders from the ZIP into your game folder.

**Good to know**
I haven't finished the game with the mod yet. Voices were assigned from the game data, so some characters may speak with the wrong gender or an unfitting voice. It's AI, so not every emphasis is perfect, especially with names.

🐛 If you notice something like that or something doesn't work, please open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/janphi001/DrovaTTAI/issues
Ideally with the character, the place in the game and the file `MelonLoader\Latest.log`.

Unofficial fan project, free. Enjoy listening! 🗡️
